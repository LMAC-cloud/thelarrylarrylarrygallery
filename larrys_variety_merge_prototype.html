STAMP ORDERS

Lar Bank: 0 LAR
Background music: off
Current rules: The generator is movable. Click it to spill a stamp into a nearby empty square. Two matching stamps merge into the next numbered stamp. At Level 4, a temporary 2× bonus becomes available. At Level 7, the generator produces a letter. Drag the Level-7 stamp onto the letter to mail it and begin a new series. When the next series reaches Level 7, its return letter becomes a second generator. The two generators are independent. The mailbox permanently destroys anything deliberately dropped onto it, except a required Level-7 stamp, which mails the series. Orders on the right reward LAR when their requested stamps are placed in their slots.
