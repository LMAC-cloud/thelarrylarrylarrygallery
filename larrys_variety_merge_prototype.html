Current rules: The brown Tristero postal bag is the generator. Click it to spill a stamp into a nearby empty square. Two matching stamps merge into the next numbered stamp. At Level 4, a temporary 2× bonus becomes available. At Level 7, the generator produces a letter. Drag the Level-7 stamp onto the letter to mail it and begin a new series. When the next series reaches Level 7, its return letter becomes a second generator. The two generators are independent. For an order, drag a requested stamp onto the red MAIL box. The stamp is sent to the first open order that needs it, and the matching box on the right becomes a green checkmark. Completing an order awards LAR.
